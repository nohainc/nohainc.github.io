Independent and interoperable

One language, matching data trees

Python and Go both pass the complete 112-case shared conformance corpus for decoding and writing. Each implementation also reads output produced by the other, including LF and CRLF documents.

The implementations are practical tools, not normative definitions of Nano Markup. The 1.0.0 specification remains the authority for syntax, data-model, and error behavior.
